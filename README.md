# FridgeChef

Take a photo of your open fridge. AI vision figures out what is in there and suggests
three dinners you can actually cook tonight, with a step-by-step recipe and what (if
anything) you are missing.

**Status: idea stage.** Nothing is designed or built yet. The concept and the questions to
settle live in [IDEA.md](IDEA.md); the design session will fill in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).

Will be inserted into the portfolio website (`patr7257/PatrickRobelWeb`,
`https://patrickrobel.dk`) once developed.
